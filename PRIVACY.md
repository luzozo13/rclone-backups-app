# Privacy policy

This app is a personal backup tool, used only by its owner.

- **What it can see:** only the files and folders it created itself in the owner's Google Drive (Google permission `drive.file`). It cannot see the owner's other files, email, contacts or any other Google data.
- **What it does:** it uploads encrypted backup files to the owner's Drive and reads them back for restore tests and restores.
- **What it collects:** nothing. No data is collected, stored elsewhere, shared, sold or used for advertising.
- **Removing its access:** the owner can remove it at any time in their Google account (Security, Third-party apps), and delete its folders in Google Drive.

Last updated: 2026-10-12.
